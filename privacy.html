Privacy Policy

Muse Youtube is a private application used only by the owner of the Restful Investor YouTube channel.

What it accesses: through the YouTube Data API, the app uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and privacy status, and reads basic channel and video information — all on the owner's own channel, and only when the owner asks it to.

What it does not do: the app does not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third party, and it does not access any other Google account or service.
